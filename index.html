(01) Latest project In production

ABEX Express

The ERP that runs an Algerian delivery company — from the first shipment to the last cash-on-delivery payout.

~5,000

69

ABEX Express is an Algerian delivery company. I design the ERP that runs its whole operation, from shipping to payment collection. It is in production and handles about 5,000 orders a day on average.

The ERP runs on a Laravel (PHP 8.3) back-end with a Vue.js back-office, connected to three mobile apps — one for customers, one for merchants and one for drivers.

(03) About

I turn ambitious ideas into software that holds up in production.

I’m Youcef Mhammdi Bouzina, a software engineer working with founders and teams to take products from a blank screen to the app stores — and then keep them fast, secure and reliable.

My work spans iOS and Android apps, Laravel and NestJS back-ends, Vue and React back-offices, the cloud and DevOps foundations underneath (AWS, Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Terraform, Prometheus, Grafana), and the AI and MLOps layer that ties it together: LLM-powered insights, secure assistants and agentic automation that feels native to the product instead of bolted on.

Today I’m designing and running the ERP behind ABEX Express, an Algerian delivery company that handles around 5,000 orders a day.
